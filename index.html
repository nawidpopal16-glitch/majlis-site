About Us

Union City's most
welcoming banqueting hall

✦

Majlis Banquet Hall is a refined gathering space designed for life's most meaningful moments. Ivory wainscoted walls, warm oak floors and beautifully set tables create an atmosphere that feels both elegant and welcoming.

From weddings and engagements to showers, birthdays, holiday gatherings, memorials and corporate functions, our dedicated team and celebrated in-house catering take care of the details — so you can simply enjoy the occasion.
